The Four Cs

Here are fifteen questions about how you lead right now. This is not a test — there is no score or benchmark to beat. What you’ll generate is a snapshot of your leadership, shaped by the four dimensions offered below.

  • CapacityThis is dependent on what you are currently carrying, what only you can carry, and what you have left at the end of a week.
  • ConfidenceThis is your ability to lead without needing all of the answers, holding on to not-knowing long enough to see things more clearly.
  • ClaritySeeing the patterns, where you fit and don’t fit, and the consequences beyond your team.
  • ConnectionHonesty without judgement within the team, and peer support from outside of the organisation.

Around six minutes. One question at a time, answered as honestly as you can manage — the reflection is only as useful as the answers are true. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. What comes back at the end is yours.
